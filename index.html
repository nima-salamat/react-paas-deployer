Pay only for what you need

With a pay-as-you-go approach, you can use the resources your application needs without committing to more capacity than necessary. Hourly usage makes it easier to test an idea, run a temporary workload, or keep a service online while your needs are still changing.

You can also move to a different resource plan when your workload grows. The goal is simple: use what you need, when you need it, and keep your deployment setup easy to understand.

Everything you need to run an application

Bring deployment, service management, CPU and memory resources, networking, and persistent storage into one focused workflow. Instead of moving between several tools for routine operations, you can create a service, deploy it, review its resources, and manage its lifecycle from one place.

Start small and scale when it matters

PassDeployer is designed for developers and teams who want a practical way to get applications running without unnecessary operational overhead. Choose a plan, create your service, and adjust resources as your workload changes.
